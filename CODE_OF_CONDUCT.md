# Code of conduct

We want this to be a place where people can ask questions, disagree and build things without being made to feel small.

**Expected**
- Be kind and assume good faith. Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Be specific and patient when reviewing, especially with newcomers.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and identity.

**Not acceptable**
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing someone's private information, or sustained unwanted contact.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or spamming issues and pull requests.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's *Report content* on the comment, or open a private security advisory on the repository and say it is a conduct report. We will read it, keep the reporter's identity private and reply.

This applies in the repository, its issues and discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.
